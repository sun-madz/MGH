The Founder

Danio Marcelin

Founder & Director, Marcelin Global Holdings

Marcelin's path into entrepreneurship started early. In 2019, while a student at La Digue School, he was one of five students chosen to represent Team Seychelles at the FIRST Global robotics challenge in Dubai — an early sign of where his interests were headed.

That interest in applied technology resurfaced in 2023 with Marcelin Robotics, an agricultural automation venture he co-founded with Nathan Guillaume, aimed at bringing low-cost robotics to Seychelles farms and easing the sector's labour pressure.

Those threads came together under Marcelin Global Holdings. Its most visible venture to date is MGH Core, a locally branded laptop line built with students and single mothers in mind, launched in June 2026 alongside business partner and shareholder Raj Kowlessur. Marcelin has since expanded into film, producing the upcoming feature Vacant Light.
